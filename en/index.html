Q&A

Frequently asked questions

How is the repair price for my laptop determined? ⌄

The exact price is determined after hardware diagnostics. The technician calls you, explains the cause and gives a fixed price for the work and parts. The repair starts only after your approval.

How do I hand over my laptop for diagnostics? ⌄

Bring it in person to 11 Parkent St. (opposite Parkent Bazaar) or send it via a delivery service (Yandex Delivery / courier).

How long does diagnostics take? ⌄

Initial hardware diagnostics usually take 40 minutes to 2 hours. For intermittent faults — up to 24 hours.

Is there a warranty on the repair? ⌄

Yes, all completed work on BGA soldering and board-level repair carries a service-centre warranty.

Do you accept devices from other regions? ⌄

Yes, we serve clients across the country — you can send the device by courier or a transport company.

How often should I clean my laptop from dust? ⌄

On average every 6–12 months; gaming models — every 6 months.
